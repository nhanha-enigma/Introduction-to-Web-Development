Our Story

North Star Bakery started in 2018 with a simple goal: bring slow-fermented, high-quality bread back to our local neighborhood. What began as a tiny kitchen experiment with a single sourdough starter has grown into a bustling storefront where neighbors gather every morning.

We believe that good baking takes time. We don't use shortcut mixes or chemical dough conditioners. Instead, we rely on long fermentation, gentle hand-shaping, and high-heat deck ovens to build real flavor and texture in every loaf.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Great bread starts with honest ingredients. We build direct relationships with regional farmers and millers to source the freshest grains available.

  • 100% Organic Flours: Our sourdoughs and baguettes use unbleached organic flour milled locally in the Pacific Northwest.
  • Local Dairy & Produce: Our butter, cream, and seasonal fruits come directly from family farms within 50 miles of our bakery.
  • No Artificial Additives: We never use artificial preservatives, dough enhancers, or synthetic colorings.
