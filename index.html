Welcome to North Star Bakery

Welcome to your local neighborhood bakery! Every morning, our team fires up the ovens before dawn to bring you fresh, handcrafted breads, delicate pastries, and custom treats. Whether you are grabbing a quick morning coffee or picking up a weekly loaf, we are proud to serve our community with ingredients sourced right from local suppliers.

What We're Known For

At North Star Bakery, we take no shortcuts. We specialize in traditional baking techniques that highlight quality and rich flavor.

  • Artisan Sourdough: Fermented for 24 hours for exceptional texture, crust, and digestability.
  • Flaky Croissants: Hand-rolled butter croissants made fresh every single morning.
  • Custom Celebration Cakes: Designed and decorated tailored to your family events and milestones.
  • Seasonal Treats: Rotating specials featuring local fruits, berries, and warm spices.
